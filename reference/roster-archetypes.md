# Reference — Roster Archetypes

Broad, employee-shaped role archetypes to draw on when proposing a roster in
Phase 3. These are **inspiration, not a menu to install wholesale** — adapt them
to what the Company Profile actually surfaced, and rename them to fit the
client's world. Each is deliberately broad (a *seat*, not a *task*).

Adapted from common OpenClaw community rosters and venture-studio team patterns
(e.g., `awesome-openclaw-agents`, CrewClaw/SwarmClaw "virtual company" kits) —
trimmed to broad employees rather than narrow specialists.

---

## Research Analyst
Investigates markets, companies, competitors, and theses. You bring a question;
it scopes, researches, and comes back with a sourced, opinionated answer. Good
first hire for almost any knowledge-work firm.

## Content Lead
Turns ideas and research into written output — posts, briefs, newsletters, decks.
Owns voice and consistency. Pairs naturally with the Research Analyst (a common
pod).

## Operations Coordinator
Keeps things moving — drafts and tracks processes, checklists, follow-ups, and
status. The "chief of staff" energy: broad, organizational, glue work.

## Deal / Opportunity Screener
For investment or BD shops: applies the firm's criteria to incoming
opportunities, produces first-pass assessments and memos with a clear
recommendation. Encodes the firm's playbook.

## Studio Lead *(only for an org/chain-of-responsibility topology)*
A coordinating role that takes a goal, delegates to specialists **in public**,
and synthesizes their work. Introduce this **only** when the profile shows a real
pipeline or hierarchy — never by default. In a library, there is no lead.

---

## How to use these

- Pick the **smallest set** that covers the surfaced problems (1–5, often fewer).
- **Rename and re-scope** to the client's domain — "Research Analyst" might
  become "Wealth-Tech Research Analyst."
- Map each chosen archetype to a problem/seat in the Company Profile. If you
  can't, don't include it.
- Decide topology separately (see `principles/agent-design.md`): most rosters are
  a **library** of these; add a pod or a Studio Lead only when earned.
