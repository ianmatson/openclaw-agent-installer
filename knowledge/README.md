# Knowledge — Drop Your Company Documents Here

Put anything you'd like your AI studio to learn from in this folder **before** (or
during) setup: theses, playbooks, process docs, brand guidelines, past memos,
FAQs, market notes — whatever defines how you work.

During onboarding, the setup assistant reads what's here and **distills** the
important parts into each agent's memory, so your agents start with real context
instead of a blank slate.

## What works well

- Plain text, Markdown, and common document formats.
- Focused, current documents over giant archives — quality beats quantity.
- A short note (even a `.txt`) explaining what a document is and why it matters.

## A few notes

- **This is not a secret store.** Don't put passwords, API keys, or credentials
  here. Those are handled separately and never committed.
- The assistant **summarizes**; it won't copy whole documents into the agents.
  Your originals stay here untouched.
- You can add more later and ask an agent to fold it in — this isn't a one-time
  window.

Nothing here is required to start. If you skip it, the studio is built from your
interview answers, and you can add documents anytime.
